# National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey

## 2013-2014 Data Documentation, Codebook, and Frequencies

### Sleep Disorders (SLQ\_H)

#### Data File: SLQ\_H.xpt

##### First Published: February 2016

##### Last Revised: NA

## Component Description

The sleep disorders (variable name prefix SLQ) data set provides a limited number of questions on sleep habits and disorders.

## Eligible Sample

Participants 16 years of age and older were eligible.

## Interview Setting and Mode of Administration

These questions were asked, in the home, by trained interviewers using the Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) system.

## Quality Assurance & Quality Control

The CAPI system is programmed with built-in consistency checks to reduce data entry errors.

## Data Processing and Editing

Edits were made to ensure completeness, consistency and analytic usefulness of the data.

## Analytic Notes

Please refer to the [NHANES Analytic Guidelines](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/analyticguidelines.aspx) and the on-line [NHANES Tutorial](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/tutorials/) for further details on the use of sample weights and other analytic issues.
